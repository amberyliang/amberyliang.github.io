About Me

I am an M.S. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at UC San Diego, specializing in machine learning and data science. My research looks at how LLM multi-agent systems fail and how to defend them, especially in medical question answering, and at how LLM agents store and retrieve memory. Before UC San Diego, I built a clinical decision-support system at Far Eastern Memorial Hospital and worked on ultrasound lesion detection at National Chung Hsing University.
